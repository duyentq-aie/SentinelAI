## Limits of this clip (recorded at the project owner's request)
The red light only exists in **0–9.3 s** and most vehicles are waiting, so this clip can only check "**no false alarm when everybody stops properly**" (the queue of waiting vehicles is not reported); **it cannot check the ability to catch real red-light runners**: there is no `red_light` label, only one vehicle passing during the red (not confirmed as a violation), and P/R/F1 cannot be computed. Measuring that needs a video with many labelled red-light-running cases (docs/LABELING_GUIDE.md).

## Design flaw of the old zone rule and the new rule `stop_line_crossing` (redesigned after the M_257 case)

**Old zone rule** (now `rules.red_light.mode: legacy_zone`, kept so that characterisation tests still run): a vehicle is reported when the bottom-centre point lies in `violation_zone` **and** the light is red in that frame. Two design flaws:
1. **It reports stationary vehicles too**: the rule only looks at position, not motion. A vehicle that stopped properly but whose front leans into the zone (or whose waiting queue bunches into the zone) is still reported as "running the light" during the whole red time. A vehicle that crossed the line while the light was still green and keeps going when the light turns red is also reported.
2. **It does not know lanes**: one shared signal for the whole zone. The right-turn/left-turn lanes have their own arrow lights (the test_video image has signs "Left-turn lane", "Right-turn lane" with their own arrow lights), but the straight-lane light is applied to them.

**M_257 example** (corrected, see EXP-023): a motorcycle **waiting, only creeping slowly up to the line** (frames 215–221, red light) is still reported as "running the light" by the old zone rule, because the box bottom-centre (y = 350) lies in `violation_zone` (y 250–380). This is exactly design flaw 1 (reporting a stationary/not-yet-crossed vehicle). By position the vehicle belongs to the `through` lane. The new rule does not report this case because the vehicle has not crossed the line.

**New rule** `stop_line_crossing` (`sentinel/rules/stopline.py`, default): a violation when the track's bottom-centre point **crosses the stop line** in the entering direction (`camera.stop_line`, `camera.direction`) **while the light of that lane is RED**. Signed distance to the line, dead zone ±`jitter_px` (4 px) against jitter; "crosses" = changes side from before the line to after the line between two observations. Not a violation: a stationary vehicle, a vehicle already past the line before the light turned red, going the opposite way, crossing while green, an `exempt` lane. At most one violation per track; the conclusion is held for `hold_s` (1 s) so that the recorder opens exactly one event. Lanes (`camera.lanes`): polygon + `signal: roi | exempt`; a vehicle belongs to the lane that contains the crossing point; with no lanes declared, the shared signal applies to the whole line (a warning in the log); a crossing point outside every lane is **not reported** and is counted (`unassigned_crossings`). The reasons are joined with " & " (for example `NO-HELMET & RED-LIGHT RUNNER`) so as not to hide the helmet/overload rules. Test: `tests/test_stopline_rule.py` (synthetic tracks, no video needed).

**(Replaced by the section "Approved geometry" below.)** State at the time of the proposal: `configs/cameras/test_video.yaml` **did not yet have** `stop_line`/`direction`/`lanes`, so the new rule was **not active** (the log warned `INACTIVE`) and the default pipeline did not report `red_light` on this clip until you approved the proposal. ## Approved geometry (2026-10-19) and the precision-first principle

The project owner approved `lanes_proposal.jpg`; the configuration is in `configs/cameras/test_video.yaml`: `stop_line` `[[400, 302], [1040, 277]]`, `direction` `[[650, 430], [665, 200]]`, three lanes (version of 2026-10-19: `left-turn` exempt, `through` roi, `right-turn` exempt; **replaced by a separate light per lane on 2026-10-20**, see the section "ROI of test_video").

**Principle** (also in `docs/PLAN.md` section 8): automated fines put precision first. When unsure, do not report, only count and log: a lane with an unclear light phase → exempt; a line-crossing point outside every lane → count `unassigned_crossings`, do not report; a rule missing `stop_line`/`direction`/`light_roi` → INACTIVE, shown on the first log line, on the HUD ("RED-LIGHT RULE INACTIVE (...)") and in the `red_light` field of `/api/status`.

## Why `light_roi` is mandatory for the `stop_line_crossing` rule (decision 2026-10-19)
The new rule does not run (INACTIVE) if the camera has no `light_roi`; it does **not** fall back to a light read by the COCO detector. Reasons: (1) on test_video, COCO sees the light in only **4/787 frames** (`UNKNOWN` in 783 frames, see the Timeline section), so with a fallback to COCO the rule would almost never see a red light and would silently report nothing; (2) the precision-first principle (`docs/PLAN.md` section 8): when the light phase is not known for sure, do not report, and that state must be clearly visible (log, HUD, `/api/status`) instead of running with an unreliable light source.

## Red-light rule using COCO tracks (`K_`) – result on test_video (after EXP-023)
The `stop_line_crossing` rule takes the **COCO** motorcycle tracks (id `K_<n>`), no longer the helmet-model tracks (see EXP-023: the helmet model almost does not see motorcycles from the stop-line area upward: 13 versus 1000+ COCO detections). The `red_light` evidence (event, images, plate search) comes from the `K_` track.
- **Linking the reason with the helmet track:** a `K_` track is linked to an `M_` track when **IoU >= 0.3 (`rules.red_light.link_iou`) or the centre of one box lies inside the other** (the helmet box is usually wider and more off-centre than the COCO box, so the centre-in-box criterion is needed); with several candidates take the highest IoU, then the closest centre. The `red_light` event records `linked: {track, reason}`, the drawn label is `ID:K_5 RED-LIGHT & NO-HELMET`. The no_helmet/overload events still belong to the `M_` track; no second `red_light` event is created for `M_`.
- **Against duplicate events when an ID breaks:** two line crossings in the **same lane**, **less than `merge_s` = 1 s** apart with crossing points **less than `merge_px` = 40 px** apart count as one event (the later one is recorded as `merged`, not reported). Both parameters are in `rules.red_light`. Limit: a track that breaks **before** the line and restarts after the line has no side change, so it is not counted as a line crossing.
- **Result** (`tools/crossings_report.py`, folder `crossings_k/`: csv/json/summary committed, images not committed): 242 `K_` tracks, **13 line crossings, 0 `red_light` events, 0 merges, 0 crossings outside every lane**. By lane and light: through/GREEN 5, right-turn/GREEN 3, **right-turn/RED 4**, left-turn/GREEN 1. 9 crossings on green were not reported because the light was green or the lane exempt. Contact sheets `crossings_1.jpg`, `crossings_2.jpg` (each tile: vehicle crop + whole frame with the line, lanes, crossing point) and `red_in_right-turn.jpg`.
- **The 4 line crossings on red in the right-turn lane: confirmed by eye (project owner, 2026-10-20).** K_21 (0.80 s), K_28 (1.67 s), K_230 (5.40 s), K_247 (7.70 s): all four vehicles **were turning right**; the right-turn arrow was green throughout the clip (0–26.2 s) so **there is no violation**. Result: the per-lane design avoided 4 false alarms that the old zone rule/the rule without lanes would report (the counter light was red at that time). Since the right-turn lane uses its own light `{roi: [845, 3, 898, 16]}` (see the section "Per-lane signals"), these 4 crossings are recorded as crossings while the lane light is GREEN (not reported, instead of "exempt lane"). The earlier rough hint about the left-turn arrow is no longer used: the left-turn lane uses the separate light of the cluster on the sign "LEFT-TURN LANE".
- **Limit:** the helmet model hardly sees motorcycles from the stop-line area upward (13 versus 1000+ COCO detections); every result here rests on COCO tracks (many broken IDs: 242 tracks), with no real labels, so this clip only proves "no false alarm" (and that tracks reach the line), not that real red-light running is caught.
- **M_257:** the old zone rule reported it falsely (a vehicle creeping while waiting for the light); the new rule is right not to report it (see the correction above and EXP-023).

## Per-lane signals (redesigned 2026-10-19)
Each lane in `camera.lanes` declares a `signal` of one of three forms:
- `{roi: [x1, y1, x2, y2]}`: **the lane's own light** (for example an arrow light), read from that region with the same HSV classifier as `light_roi` (`sentinel/lights.py`), unchanged;
- `"shared"`: use the shared `camera.light_roi` (the old spelling `"roi"` is understood as `"shared"`);
- `"exempt"`: the lane has no light, the red-light rule does not apply.
The rule decides a violation by the state of the light governing the lane that contains the crossing point: a lane with its own light that reads `UNKNOWN` is **not reported** (precision first). The state of each lane is recorded in: the log (`[lights] lane 'left-turn' signal RED -> GREEN` at every change, and a line declaring the lanes at start-up), the HUD (the line `LANE SIGNALS: ...`), and `/api/status` (`red_light.lane_signals`: `{signal, roi, state}` for each lane). The "light" column in the line-crossing list is the state of the light governing that lane. Test: `tests/test_lane_signals.py` (all three forms, the old form, invalid forms, light reading in the pipeline, log, HUD, status).

## Limits (signals and light ROI)
- **The light ROIs are small and close to the top edge of the frame:** the light ROIs are only 15–22 px high and lie at the top edge of the frame (y from 0). If the camera shifts (wind, a bump, readjustment) the ROI will point at the wrong place and read the wrong state. **There is no automatic check yet.** A later direction (not done): at start-up match (template matching) the positions of the light clusters against a stored reference image, and set the rule to INACTIVE (clearly shown in the log/HUD/`/api/status`) when the offset exceeds a threshold.
- **The right-turn arrow is never red in the clip** (green throughout 0–26.2 s), so the **red case of the right-turn lane has not been checked**: it is not known how the HSV reader handles a red arrow of this cluster, and the rule has never reported a violation in this lane.
- **The left-turn arrow flickers `UNKNOWN` while green** (nearly white core, only 1–44 green pixels, below/around the classifier threshold): **harmless**, because `UNKNOWN` never produces a violation (precision first); only the RED state produces violations and RED of this cluster is very clear (150–185 red pixels).
- Other limits of the clip: no real labels, it only proves "no false alarm" (see the sections above).
